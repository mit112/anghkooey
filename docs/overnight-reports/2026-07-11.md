- **#50 sync restart UX — launch-time preference SNAPSHOT, not `SyncMode`.** `AppState.launchSyncPreferenceEnabled` captures `SyncPreference.isEnabled` at launch; Settings compares it to the live toggle to show effective-vs-pending ("On" / "Off" / "On after you restart" / "Off after you restart"), with persistent inline restart instructions + a one-time alert on entering the pending state. Chose the snapshot over the derived `SyncMode` because on the Simulator `SyncMode` is always `.local` and would mislead. Restart-not-hot-swap documented in ADR-0011. **IN main.**

- **GPT-5.5 checkpoints caught THREE real bugs, all fixed before merge:**
  1. **#38** — the `.anghkooeyDeckDidChange` notification was posted *inside* the load-generation guard, so a successful delete superseded by a concurrent `loadDueQueue()` never refreshed the widget / pruned other queues; and the generation bump wasn't restored on a failed delete (could strand a concurrent load in `.loading`).   3. **#35/#36** — reviewers flagged a concurrent-double-tap append race; the checkpoint **downgraded** it (pre-existing — the old intent appended unconditionally; this adds a guard; reconcile computes from one cached pre-state → last-wins + one phantom log, bounded harm) and endorsed two cheap fixes that were applied: the local advance-write is logged (not `try?`-swallowed), and `appendGrade` now uses an atomic `O_APPEND` write so racing appends can't interleave into a silently-dropped line.
